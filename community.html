What it is

Share experiences, photos and plans around VAIGA events. Reply to each other – with one level of answers, so conversations stay clear.

Draft

Community rules

Draft – to be checked by a lawyer before the community opens.

  1. What it is for. The VAIGA Community is a closed meeting place for approved members around VAIGA events: sharing experiences, photos and plans, and meeting up before an event (Meet & Go).
  2. What it is not. Not a dating site, not a general social network. There are no private messages, likes, member search, profiles or friend requests.
  3. Membership. By invitation only, 18 years or older. An administrator approves every request. Other members only see your first name and the initial of your last name.
  4. Respect. No harassment, discrimination, hate, threats or sexual content. No advertising or requests for private contact.
  5. Photos. Only share photos you may share, and ask people before you post a photo of them.
  6. Meet & Go. Only at a public place, linked to an upcoming VAIGA event. No phone numbers, e-mail addresses or links in the meeting point. Only participants see who is going.
  7. Reporting and moderation. Everyone can report a post, reply or Meet & Go. Moderators can remove content and block access; the author gets the reason.
  8. Your data. See the privacy policy. You can ask to delete your account and all your community data at any time.
