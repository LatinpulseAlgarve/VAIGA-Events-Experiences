About VAIGA

Let's go.
Let's celebrate.

VAIGA is an international event organisation bringing people together through music, entertainment and unforgettable experiences.

Our mission

Experiences that connect people

Music has the power to bring people together – across ages, backgrounds and countries. VAIGA turns that power into events: nights and festivals where people meet, dance, celebrate and make memories together.

As an overarching event brand, VAIGA develops and organises different event concepts, each with its own identity – from Latin parties and disco & retro nights to 40+ parties, festivals, themed parties, special events and live music.
