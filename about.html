About VAIGA

Let's go.
Let's celebrate.

VAIGA is an international event organisation bringing people together through music, entertainment and unforgettable experiences.

VAIGA is all about discovering, connecting and experiencing new things.

The name VAIGA combines two languages: “Vai” in Portuguese and “Ga” in Dutch. Both express the same feeling: Let's go!

And that's exactly what VAIGA stands for: encouraging people to get out there, explore new places, discover businesses, enjoy events and experience everything life has to offer.

VAIGA brings people, businesses, services, events, music and experiences together on one platform. Whether you're looking for inspiration, new opportunities, entertainment or somewhere new to explore, VAIGA helps you find it.

We believe life is about discovering, connecting and experiencing more.

Don't just stay. Explore. Discover. Experience.

Our mission

Experiences that connect people

Music has the power to bring people together – across ages, backgrounds and countries. VAIGA turns that power into events: nights and parties where people meet, dance, celebrate and make memories together. As an overarching event brand, VAIGA develops and organises different event concepts, each with its own identity – from Latin parties and disco & retro nights to 40+ parties, themed parties, special events and live music.
